Overview

What this actually covers.

Every business accumulates manual work: copying data between systems, sending the same email over and over, chasing status updates. Individually small, collectively enormous.

We identify those loops and close them — with straightforward automation where that's enough, and with AI where genuine language understanding is needed. The test is always the same: does it save real hours or reduce real errors? If not, we don't build it.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need AI, or just automation?
Often just automation — it's cheaper, more predictable, and easier to maintain. AI earns its place when the task needs understanding language or handling genuine ambiguity. We'll tell you which applies.
Will this work with our existing tools?
Almost certainly. Most modern business tools offer APIs or integration platforms. We confirm compatibility during process mapping, before you commit.
Is our data safe?
We scope data handling explicitly at the start — what's processed, where it's stored, and who can access it. Sensitive workflows can be built to keep data entirely within your infrastructure.
What if the automation breaks?
Everything ships with monitoring and alerting. Retainer clients get fixes handled directly; project clients get documentation to handle it in-house.
Can you add AI to our existing product?
Yes — that's a common request. We integrate into your current stack rather than asking you to rebuild around it.
