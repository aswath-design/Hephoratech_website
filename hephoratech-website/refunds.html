Refund & Cancellation Policy

How Vendra subscriptions are billed, how to cancel one, and when we refund a payment.

Last updated 16 September 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to subscriptions to Vendra, our Windows billing and point-of-sale software, bought from HephoraTech through this website or from inside the Vendra application. Project work — websites, apps and other services we are engaged to build — is governed by our Terms & Conditions instead.

HephoraTech is based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India. All prices are in Indian rupees (INR).

2. Free trial

Every Vendra plan starts with a 7-day free trial. No card is needed to begin the trial, and nothing is charged during it. You are charged only when you choose a paid plan and complete the payment.

3. How billing works

Vendra is sold as a subscription, billed in advance either monthly or yearly. The plan you choose and the amount payable are shown before you pay, and the same figures appear on your receipt.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period at the then-current price for your plan, until you cancel. If we change a price, we will tell you before the change applies to your renewal.

4. Payments and Razorpay

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. Card, UPI, net banking and wallet details are entered on Razorpay's secure payment page and are handled by Razorpay under their terms and privacy policy. HephoraTech does not receive or store your full card number or your UPI credentials.

We receive from Razorpay only what we need to run your account: the payment status, the amount, the payment reference, and the plan it relates to.

5. When your access starts

Vendra is delivered as software, not as a physical product, so there is nothing to ship. Once a payment succeeds, your plan is activated on your account, usually within a few minutes. If a successful payment has not activated your plan within 24 hours, contact us and we will fix it or refund the payment in full.

6. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time by writing to info@hephoratech.com from the email address on the account, or by asking us through the Vendra owner menu.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period you have already paid for. Vendra keeps working normally until then, and you are not billed again afterwards. We do not charge a cancellation fee.

7. Refunds

New subscriptions. If Vendra is not right for your shop, write to us within 7 days of your first paid charge and we will refund that charge in full. This applies once per customer, on the first payment for a plan.

Renewals and part-periods. Renewal charges and unused parts of a billing period are not normally refunded, because access continues for the whole period you paid for. If a renewal caught you by surprise — for example you meant to cancel and told us shortly afterwards — write to us and we will look at it fairly.

Payment problems. Duplicate charges, an amount that does not match the plan you chose, or a charge for a plan that was never activated are refunded in full, whenever they are reported.

If we cannot deliver. If we withdraw a plan or cannot continue providing the service you have paid for, we refund the unused part of your current billing period.

8. How to request a refund

Email info@hephoratech.com with the email address on the account, the payment reference from your receipt, and a line about what went wrong. We reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds are sent back through Razorpay to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. How quickly it then appears depends on your bank or card issuer.

9. Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Razorpay retries it and we tell you inside the application. Your shop data is never deleted because of a failed payment. If the payment is not completed, the subscription lapses and Vendra returns to its unpaid state — your data stays on your computer and becomes available again when a payment succeeds.

10. Your data after cancellation

Vendra stores your shop data on your own Windows computer. Cancelling a subscription does not delete it, and you can continue to take backups and CSV exports of your records. Any account information we hold is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

11. Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank — it is almost always faster. We will work with you and with Razorpay to resolve genuine payment errors.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products and payment arrangements change. The revision date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. The version in force when you paid is the one that applies to that payment.
