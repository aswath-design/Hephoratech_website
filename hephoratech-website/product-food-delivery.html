Why It Exists

The problem it solves.

Listing on third-party delivery apps costs you 20–30% of every order, hides your customer data, and puts your brand next to a dozen competitors on the same screen.

A custom platform flips that. Customers order in your app, you keep the margin and the data, and your kitchen and riders run on tools built around how you actually operate — not a generic marketplace workflow.

Built for cloud kitchens and traditional kitchens alike. If you run a cloud kitchen, the platform is designed for exactly that model — multiple virtual brands operating out of one physical kitchen, each with its own menu, pricing, and storefront, but all orders landing in a single prep queue so your staff work from one screen. If you run a traditional dine-in restaurant, sweet stall, bakery, tiffin centre, or a small family kitchen, the same system handles takeaway and delivery alongside your counter business, with menus you can switch by time of day for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

It scales down as comfortably as it scales up. A single-outlet kitchen can run it with one tablet in the kitchen and one rider account, while a growing chain can add branches, delivery zones, and staff logins without changing systems. Because we build and maintain it ourselves, features get added around how your kitchen actually works — not how a template thinks it should.

How It Works

From order to doorstep.

  1. Customer orders

    Browses your menu, customises items, applies offers, and pays in your branded app.

  2. Kitchen accepts

    The order lands on the restaurant dashboard with a prep timer and printed ticket.

  3. Rider assigned

    The system picks the nearest available rider and routes them to the pickup point.

  4. Delivered & tracked

    The customer follows the map live, then rates the order — feedback lands in your dashboard.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do we own the app?
Yes. It is published under your own developer accounts and branded entirely as yours. You own the product and the customer data it collects.
How long does it take to launch?
A standard build — customer app, restaurant dashboard, and rider app — typically takes 8 to 12 weeks depending on payment integrations and how custom your workflow is.
Which payment methods are supported?
UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, popular wallets, and cash on delivery. We integrate the payment gateway that gives you the best rates.
Can it handle multiple branches?
Yes. Each outlet can have its own menu, pricing, delivery radius, and staff logins, while you see consolidated reporting across all of them.
What about ongoing support?
We maintain the platform after launch — monitoring, updates, OS compatibility, and new features as your operation grows.
