Our Story

Why we exist.

There's a gap between "we need a website" and "we need a real digital business." Plenty of agencies will build you the first. Far fewer will tell you what the second actually requires.

HephoraTech was started to close that gap — one team covering design, development, e-commerce, and growth, so clients aren't left coordinating four vendors who each blame the others.

We work with two kinds of clients: businesses that need custom development, e-commerce, or growth marketing, and institutions like schools that need dependable software to run daily operations. Different problems, same requirement — software that works and someone who answers the phone.
