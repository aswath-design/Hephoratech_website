Overview

What this actually covers.

Posting consistently is hard, and posting without a plan rarely produces anything. We handle both halves — the creative that gets made and the strategy that decides what's worth making.

On the paid side, we run campaigns judged on cost per enquiry and return on ad spend. Reach and impressions are inputs, not results. If a campaign isn't producing leads, we change it rather than reporting around it.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How many posts per month?
Typically 12–20 depending on the plan and formats. We'd rather ship fewer strong posts than fill a calendar with filler.
Do I need to be in the videos?
No. Plenty of clients never appear on camera — product footage, motion graphics, and text-led formats work well. That said, founder-led content usually performs better if you're open to it.
What ad budget should I start with?
Enough to gather meaningful data — usually a modest daily spend over 2–4 weeks. We'll recommend a figure based on your market and margins rather than a generic number.
Who owns the content?
You do. All assets are delivered as source files you keep, whether or not we continue working together.
Can you manage the accounts entirely?
Yes — posting, scheduling, comment monitoring, and message response can all be handled as part of the retainer.
