Overview

What this actually covers.

SEO gets sold as a mystery. It isn't. Search engines need to crawl your site, understand what each page is about, and see that it loads quickly on a phone. Most sites fail at least one of those, and traffic suffers accordingly.

We start with an audit that tells you exactly what's broken and what it's costing you — then fix it. If your site is fundamentally slow or badly structured, we'll say so rather than charging you monthly to optimize something that needs rebuilding.

What's included

  • Technical SEO audit

    Crawlability, indexing, sitemaps, robots.txt, canonicals, redirects, and structured data.

  • On-page optimization

    Titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, and content gaps.

  • Site speed

    Core Web Vitals, image optimization, script cleanup, caching, and hosting review.

  • Existing website optimization

    Rebuilds and targeted fixes for sites that look fine but underperform.

  • Local SEO

    Google Business Profile, local citations, and location pages — relevant if you serve a region.

  • Reporting

    Plain-language monthly reports on rankings, traffic, and what changed.

How It Works

Our process.

Every engagement follows the same shape, so you always know what stage we're at.

  1. Audit

    A full technical and content crawl, benchmarked against competitors in your space.

  2. Priority list

    Findings ranked by impact versus effort — you see what matters most before we start.

  3. Fixes

    Technical corrections, speed work, and on-page changes implemented and verified.

  4. Content & structure

    Improvements to page structure and internal linking so authority flows properly.

  5. Monitor

    Ongoing tracking of rankings, traffic, and Core Web Vitals with monthly reporting.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How long before I see results?
Technical fixes and speed gains show up within weeks. Ranking movement typically takes 3–6 months depending on competition — anyone promising faster is guessing.
Do you guarantee page one?
No, and be cautious of anyone who does. We guarantee the work: a technically sound, fast, well-structured site that gives you the best possible shot.
Is the audit useful on its own?
Yes. Plenty of clients take the audit and hand it to their own developer. It's a standalone deliverable, not a sales pitch.
Can you fix a site you didn't build?
That's most of this work. We audit and improve existing sites on any platform — WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, or custom.
What about AI search and answer engines?
Structured data, clear content hierarchy, and fast pages help across both traditional search and AI-driven results. We build for both.
