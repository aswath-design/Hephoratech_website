Overview

What this actually covers.

Most websites fail for boring reasons — they load slowly, they break on phones, and nobody can find them. We build the opposite: fast, responsive, search-friendly sites and apps that hold up under real traffic.

Whether you need a marketing site that converts visitors into enquiries, an internal web app that replaces a pile of spreadsheets, or a mobile app your customers actually keep installed, we handle design, development, testing, and deployment as one continuous piece of work.

How It Works

Our process.

Every engagement follows the same shape, so you always know what stage we're at.

  1. Requirements & scope

    We map what the product must do, who uses it, and what success looks like — then write it down so nothing drifts.

  2. Wireframes & design

    Layout and UI design you approve before a line of production code gets written.

  3. Development

    Built in reviewable milestones so you see working software early, not just at the end.

  4. Testing & QA

    Cross-browser, cross-device, and load testing before anything reaches your users.

  5. Deploy & handover

    Production deployment, domain and SSL setup, plus documentation and a walkthrough for your team.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How long does a website take?
A focused marketing site typically runs 3–5 weeks. Web apps and mobile apps depend on feature count — we give a firm timeline after scoping, not before.
Do I own the code?
Yes. Everything we build for you is yours, including source code, design files, and hosting accounts.
Can you work with my existing site?
Absolutely — see our website optimization service if the site exists but underperforms. We can also rebuild in stages so you're never offline.
What technologies do you use?
We pick based on the job rather than habit — modern JavaScript frameworks for apps, lighter stacks for content sites, and native or cross-platform tooling for mobile.
Do you provide hosting?
We can set up and manage hosting for you, or deploy to infrastructure you already own.
