    Good questions

    A little clarity before you start.

    Want to talk about your particular setup?

    Talk to Hephora Tech
    Does Vendra work without internet?
    Core billing, products, customers and reports are available locally on your Windows computer. Online account services may still need a connection.
    Can I use my existing scanner and printer?
    Vendra is designed for barcode scanning and ESC/POS receipt printing on Windows. Share your equipment models with Hephora Tech and we'll confirm your setup.
    Can I start with my own products?
    Yes. Start with an empty catalogue and add your products, prices and opening stock. Sample items are there if you'd rather explore first.
    Does Vendra support GST on bills?
    Vendra calculates GST and shows the tax split on screen and on the printed bill. You configure your shop and products with the details that apply to your business.
    Where does my shop data live?
    On the Windows computer running Vendra. You can choose a backup folder and create additional backups whenever you want.
    What happens after the 7-day trial?
    Nothing is deleted. Your shop data stays where it is, and you choose a plan when you're ready to carry on billing.
