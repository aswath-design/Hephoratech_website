Overview

What this actually covers.

An online store is not a brochure. Every element — product page layout, checkout flow, image loading, trust signals — either helps someone buy or gets in their way. We build and optimize stores with that lens.

We work across Shopify for owned storefronts, Amazon for marketplace reach, and custom builds when neither fits. If you're already selling, we audit the funnel and fix what's leaking revenue instead of rebuilding for the sake of it.

How It Works

Our process.

Every engagement follows the same shape, so you always know what stage we're at.

  1. Catalogue & platform review

    We look at what you sell, how it's categorized, and which platform genuinely fits.

  2. Store design

    Storefront and product page design built for scanning and buying, not just browsing.

  3. Build & integrations

    Theme development plus payments, shipping, inventory, and analytics connected.

  4. Test orders

    Full checkout runs on real devices before launch — including failure cases.

  5. Launch & optimize

    Go live, then track behaviour and tune the funnel over the first weeks.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Shopify or a custom store?
Shopify for most businesses — it handles payments, security, and scale so you don't have to. Custom makes sense when you need workflows or integrations Shopify genuinely can't support.
Can you migrate my existing store?
Yes, including products, customers, and order history. We plan migrations to avoid downtime and preserve SEO rankings.
Do you handle Amazon listings?
We set up storefronts, A+ content, and catalogue structure. Ongoing listing management can be arranged as a retainer.
What about payment gateways in India?
We integrate Razorpay, PayU, Stripe, PayPal, and Shopify Payments depending on where you sell and who your buyers are.
Will my store be fast?
Speed is a conversion issue, so yes — image optimization, lazy loading, and app auditing are part of every build.
