Common Questions

Before you write.

What information should I include?
What you're trying to achieve, any deadline you're working to, and roughly what budget range you have in mind. That's enough for us to come back with something useful rather than a generic reply.
How quickly do you respond?
Usually the same working day, and always within 24 hours on weekdays.
Do you work with clients outside Tiruppur?
Yes — we're based in Tamil Nadu but work with clients across India and internationally. Everything runs remotely with scheduled calls at times that suit you.
Do you charge for the first conversation?
No. Initial discussion and scoping are free. You'll get a written proposal with fixed scope and price before anything is committed.
What if I'm not sure what I need?
That's a normal starting point. Describe the problem rather than the solution — working out what should actually be built is part of what we do.
