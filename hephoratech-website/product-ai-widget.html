Why It Exists

The problem it solves.

Most website visitors have a question the page doesn’t quite answer — and they have it at 11pm, on a weekend, or two minutes before they leave for a competitor. A contact form replies tomorrow. A phone number goes to voicemail. And staffing live chat around the clock is expensive.

The AI Widget closes that gap. It reads your own website, documents and FAQs, then answers visitors in your voice, instantly, day or night — grounded in what your business actually says, not a generic chatbot guessing. When a visitor wants a real person, or the conversation is worth a human, it rings a teammate’s phone and a person picks up right where the bot left off.

The result. Fewer questions go unanswered, more visitors turn into enquiries with a name and number attached, and your team only steps in for the conversations that need them — instead of watching a chat box all day.

Who it's for

Service businesses

Answer pricing, availability and “do you do X?” questions before the visitor leaves.

SaaS & product sites

Instant support from your docs, with a human handoff for the hard ones.

Local & e-commerce

Capture leads and orders after hours, straight to WhatsApp or your inbox.

How It Works

From your content to a real conversation.

Five moving parts, and you only ever touch the first one.

1 · Learns your content

We crawl your website, docs and FAQs into a private knowledge base. Update your site, and the widget keeps up.

2 · Answers instantly

Every reply is grounded in your own material — so it’s accurate to your business, not a generic guess — and it comes back in seconds.

3 · Captures the visitor

A light name-and-number step means every chat is a lead you can follow up — not an anonymous session that vanishes.

4 · Rings a human

When a visitor asks for a person, our agent app rings a teammate’s phone like a call. They accept and take over the live chat.

5 · Fires your automations

New leads flow onward through n8n — email, WhatsApp, your CRM or Slack — so nothing sits waiting to be noticed.

One-line install

A single script tag drops it onto any site. It renders in an isolated Shadow DOM, so it never clashes with your existing styles.

The Companion App

Answer chats from your phone.

The widget is only half of it. Every AI Widget comes with a mobile agent app for your team — so a real person can jump into any conversation from wherever they are.

Sign in with Google, and you see the team’s live queue — all chats, unread, and the ones waiting for a person. Open any conversation to read its full history, take over from the bot, and reply with text, a photo, or a voice note. When a visitor asks for a human, the phone rings full-screen like an incoming call; accept it and you’re in.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How does it know about my business?
We crawl your website, documents and FAQs into a private knowledge base for your widget. Its answers come from that material, so they stay accurate to your business. When your content changes, we refresh what it knows.
What happens when the AI can’t answer?
It hands off to a person. A visitor can ask for a human at any time, and your team gets a full-screen ring on the agent app. They accept and take over the live chat — with the whole conversation already in front of them.
How hard is it to install?
One line. You add a single script tag to your site and it appears. It renders inside an isolated Shadow DOM, so it won’t clash with your existing design or code — and there’s no build step on your end.
Does it work on any website?
Yes — WordPress, Shopify, a custom site, a landing page, anything that lets you add a script tag. The same widget you see on this page is the product.
Can we connect it to WhatsApp or our CRM?
Yes. New leads and conversations can flow through n8n into WhatsApp, email, your CRM or Slack — so follow-up happens where your team already works.
Who owns the conversations and data?
You do. Conversations, leads and the knowledge base are yours. We run it for you and can walk you through exactly what is stored and where.
