Why It Exists

The problem it solves.

Most schools run on a patchwork: admissions in one system, fees in a spreadsheet, attendance on paper, and communication over WhatsApp. Nothing reconciles, and staff spend their time moving data between systems instead of teaching.

School Manager brings it into one platform. Admissions, fees, attendance, timetables, grades, and parent communication share the same records — so a student's information is entered once and correct everywhere.

How it’s designed to work day to day. The moment a parent submits an admission enquiry, the record exists — and it follows that student for the rest of their time with you. Fees are raised against the same record, receipts are generated automatically, and pending dues show up on a live list instead of being discovered at the end of term. Teachers mark attendance from a phone or a class terminal in seconds, absences trigger a parent notification the same morning, and marks entered into the gradebook roll straight into report cards. The aim is simple: office staff stop chasing paper and start seeing the whole picture on one screen.

The second focus is what it gives your leadership. Because everything sits in one database, the reports write themselves — collection against target, class-wise attendance trends, teacher workload, admission conversion from enquiry to enrolment, and year-on-year comparisons. Principals and management should get answers in a few clicks rather than waiting days for someone to compile a spreadsheet. And because parents get fee reminders, attendance alerts, and results through the same system, the number of phone calls your front desk fields every day drops noticeably.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can we start with just some modules?
Yes — it's modular. Many schools begin with admissions and fees, then add attendance and academics once staff are comfortable.
How do parents access it?
Through a mobile-friendly parent portal. Notifications go out over email and SMS, so parents don't need to log in to stay informed.
Can we migrate existing student data?
Yes. We handle migration from spreadsheets or an existing system as part of onboarding, including verification that nothing was lost.
Does it handle multiple branches?
Yes — multi-branch institutions can manage each location separately while retaining consolidated reporting at the top.
What about training our staff?
Onboarding includes staff training and documentation. Support continues after go-live rather than ending at handover.
